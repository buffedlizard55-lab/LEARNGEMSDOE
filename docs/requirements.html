Catalogue attribute list — measured, not estimated

The highest-leverage requirement is to read how the catalogue was built, not only what it looks like. The zip that holds the field-definition text could not be downloaded from this machine (GDR file, TLS failure). The same 2023-06-27 compilation is published as a public map service by the Nevada Bureau of Mines and Geology. Counts below are returnCountOnly responses from that service on 2026-09-26. Click a count to re-run the query.

Scope flag. The service describes “Quaternary fault traces, slip and recency for the DoE INGENIOUS (EE0009254) study area. Updated 06-27-2023.” That is the regional compilation rules §3.3 says the training labels were obtained from. It is not the competition label raster, and these counts are not a pixel count. This session did not clip the service to the GeoDAWN outline. Do not use 22,956 as the number of competition labels.
