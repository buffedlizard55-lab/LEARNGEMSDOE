GV-1 · Eligibility (Official Rules §1.3) vs homepage short text

verified discrepancy

Homepage (hub): individual competitors must be U.S. citizens or permanent residents; teams must have a captain who is a U.S. citizen or permanent resident; private entities incorporated in and with primary place of business in the U.S.; federal entities and federal employees not eligible.

Official Rules §1.3 additional points (PDF, fetched 2026-09-26):

  • Team members other than the captain: eligible if “legally authorized to work in the United States.”
  • Academic institutions must be U.S.-based and nationally accredited.
  • FFRDCs may not compete; individual FFRDC researchers may compete in individual capacity without FFRDC resources, honorable mention only, no cash.
  • Non-DOE federal entities and federal employees: not eligible for any portion.
  • DrivenData officers/employees/judges and household/family: ineligible.
  • DOE employees and DOE support-service contractors, sponsoring-organization employees, and their families/households: ineligible.
  • Debarred persons: ineligible. Under 18 / under age of majority: ineligible.
  • Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Program participants (FCOC-sponsored) and their teams: ineligible. FCOC list in footnote 2: Iran, North Korea, Russia, Belarus, and China (subject to change).
  • Entities owned/controlled by an FCOC government: ineligible.

Irregularity: Forum thread 11540 (dmitry_v, ~2 days before fetch) asks which text controls for non-captain team members. No organizer reply was present at fetch. Treat the PDF as controlling until staff say otherwise; do not add ineligible members.

Related unanswered: maksimovka, 10 Sep 2026 — one US resident + one non-US resident team?

GV-2 · Generative AI disclosure (rules §3.2) — why this log exists

verified confidence: high

PDF §3.2: generative AI is allowed in developing the prize submission, but competitors must indicate in the narrative (not in the word count) the extent of use and how it was used. Competitors own accuracy, authenticity, and authorship. Risks named: fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism. Running log: AI-usage log.

GV-3 · Submission mechanics

verified

  • One GeoTIFF, 100 m, EPSG:32611, float32 [0,1], same bounds as training, outside bounds null/NaN — format.
  • Up to three submissions per week; choose one final for both prize rounds without seeing private scores — PDF §3.2, §3.4, §3.6.2.
  • Reset is a rolling window, not a calendar week — staff 17 Sep 2026.
  • This research agent does not consume those slots.
  • Finalists must later provide complete code and docs sufficient to reproduce and to generate predictions on new samples — §3.2 / §3.5.
  • PDF §A.1 also says submissions must be posted by 5:00 p.m. ET on the deadline date. Hub lists 3 Dec 2026, 11:59 p.m. UTC. Those are not the same clock time. unresolved which clock wins for the GeoTIFF upload — flag, do not pick one.

GV-4 · External data

verified

Problem description: additional data allowed if the team has a license that permits use in this challenge and sharing with the sponsor for evaluation (external datasets). Staff repeated that sentence for paid subscriptions (11528). Hub: spatial overlap of train (USGS Q faults) and test (new faults) is allowed; train on the provided faults; evaluation is on the private new-fault set.

GV-5 · Scoring mask (staff, binding for strategy)

verified confidence: high

Thread 11516:

  • Known USGS/INGENIOUS pixels are masked/excluded from evaluation in both rounds and do not contribute to the penalty terms (staff 11516).
  • The 300 m triangular kernel measures distance to new-fault ground truth, not a buffer around known faults; a prediction beside a known trace but far from any new-fault pixel is still fully penalized.
  • Staff did not explicitly state the exact masking implementation (pixel-exact vs. a small buffer) in the fetched posts. The provided training-label raster encodes the known faults, so the practical effect is the same for strategy — but treat "pixel-exact" as an inference, not a staff quote.
  • New-fault pixels may lie within 300 m of known traces (corrections/modifications). Identifying those is an intended outcome and some may already be in the new-fault set.

GV-7 · Prize structure (do not confuse rounds)

verified

Initial: top 5 on private test each $10,000 ($50,000). Final: same submissions rescored on expanded labels after expert review of all teams — $100k / $70k / $40k / $25k / $15k. Hub diagram and structure section. PDF §1.1 matches those dollar amounts. PDF also: “All Phase 1 competitors will be eligible to compete in Phase 2 and will be automatically submitted for consideration.” PDF §A.13: the prize judge may apply program policy factors (DOE priorities, geographic diversity, U.S. employment/manufacturing, and others) in addition to reviewer scores. DTI rank is not guaranteed to be the sole winner-selection input.

If the team changes, re-read §1.3 before any modeling work is attributed to a new person.
